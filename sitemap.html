THE COMPANY

Technology should make the room easier to live in.

Haptique Electronics Pvt. Ltd. is the company behind ROOT. We are building hardware and software that brings practical intelligence closer to the people and places that use it.

ROOT is our first focused product: a local thermostat for split air conditioners, designed for Indian homes and everyday comfort.
