# ACBOSS Home and Product Content Allocation

## Decision

The homepage creates recognition and emotional relevance. The product page is the complete decision-making surface. No section is duplicated: detailed operating, installation, technical, comparison, and support material moves to the product page.

## Homepage

Keep the wall-mounted hero, concise bed-level comfort problem, sleep-story carousel, three high-level benefits, a compact product handoff, and a direct product-page CTA. Remove the setup walkthrough, mounting and power details, architecture inspector, simulator, comparison, interest form, and full FAQ.

## Product page

Keep the existing product hero and gallery. Add the complete problem explanation, setup walkthrough, mounting and power details, architecture inspector, simulator, comparison, canonical FAQ, and contact form. The contact form remains a mailto enquiry, never a fake reservation confirmation.

## Constraints

- Public wordmark is `ACBOSS`.
- Preserve existing ACBOSS copy and assets; relocate rather than delete detailed material.
- Preserve gallery, carousel, inspector, and simulator behavior.
- Keep reduced-motion safeguards and usable mobile layout.
