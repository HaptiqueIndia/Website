# AC Boss Wall-Mounted Render

## Purpose

Create a landing-page-ready hero image that demonstrates AC Boss mounted on a clean interior wall.

## Visual direction

- Photorealistic, premium landing-page product image with the clean, front-on wall-mounted composition of the supplied visual reference.
- AC Boss is mounted front-facing and centered on a pale warm-grey interior wall, with the black rounded-square form, central white LED, lower black band, and `ACBOSS` wordmark matching the retained reference PNG.
- Use a soft natural daylight gradient and a delicate cast shadow/halo behind the unit to establish depth without a visible bracket.
- Keep a spacious, uncluttered composition with no browser chrome, competitor branding, headline copy, or surrounding décor.

## Constraints

- No people, UI overlays, marketing copy, watermarks, or added product controls.
- Keep the AC Boss product visually faithful; do not change its materials, silhouette, LED count, or branding.
- Use the existing AC Boss Product Render ImageGen template as the hardware reference.

## Success criteria

The final image clearly communicates wall mounting, appears credible as premium product photography, and is suitable for use as a clean website hero.
