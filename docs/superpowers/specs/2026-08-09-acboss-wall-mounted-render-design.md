# AC Boss Wall-Mounted Render

## Purpose

Create a landing-page-ready hero image that demonstrates AC Boss mounted on a bedroom wall near a split air conditioner.

## Visual direction

- Photorealistic, premium 16:9 bedroom scene at night.
- AC Boss is mounted front-facing beneath the split AC, with the black rounded-square form, central white LED, lower black band, and `ACBOSS` wordmark matching the retained reference PNG.
- Warm bedside lighting and restrained cool room tones create a calm sleep-focused atmosphere.
- Maintain quieter negative space on the left for site copy.

## Constraints

- No people, UI overlays, marketing copy, watermarks, or added product controls.
- Keep the AC Boss product visually faithful; do not change its materials, silhouette, LED count, or branding.
- Use the existing AC Boss Product Render ImageGen template as the hardware reference.

## Success criteria

The final image clearly communicates wall mounting and AC proximity, appears credible as product photography, and is suitable for use as a website hero without obscuring text placed on the left.
