# AC Boss Product Page Layout

## Purpose

Rebuild the AC Boss product-detail page with the information architecture and visual pacing of the supplied tado° product page, while keeping all Acboss copy, branding, CSS, assets, and interactions original.

## Reference-derived layout method

1. Minimal product navigation and a spacious, image-led hero with one product promise and one primary CTA.
2. Three concise benefit blocks directly beneath the hero.
3. Alternating full-width product visuals and short explanatory sections to explain the product, mounting, sensing, and setup.
4. A choice/compatibility section that makes the product path easy to understand rather than presenting an exhaustive technical data sheet early.
5. A smart-home compatibility/proof band.
6. An expandable feature or FAQ section, followed by a final CTA and support links.

## AC Boss expression

- Use the canonical black AC Boss controller for every product view.
- Use the wall-mounted render as the hero or installation visual and the technical cutaway as an explicitly illustrative technology visual.
- Retain AC Boss sleep-comfort positioning; replace the current terminal/telemetry voice with calm, plain household language.
- Preserve only claims that can be supported or explicitly mark future/illustrative capabilities.
- Use original warm-grey, off-white, charcoal, and restrained coral accents. Do not reuse tado° assets, copy, selector names, or CSS.

## Constraints

- Keep the static HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture unless implementation requires a small, self-contained interaction helper.
- Maintain keyboard-accessible accordions, gallery controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts.
- Do not promise checkout or reservation completion unless the flow is connected to a real endpoint.

## Success criteria

The page immediately identifies AC Boss as the product for sale, follows the reference page's clear visual sequence, stays visually distinct from tado°, and supports confident mobile and desktop scanning.
