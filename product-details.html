ENGINEERING THESIS

Why Your AC Overcools & How ACBOSS Solves It

Understand the built-in AC thermostat flaw, wired thermostat barriers, and how ACBOSS creates continuous real-world room comfort.

PROBLEM 1 // THERMAL PHYSICS FLAW

The Thermostat Built in the AC

Even a 24°C AC setting feels freezing cold after running for a while. Why? Because the thermostat built in the AC measures return air near the ceiling, not your bed.

There is a 3°C to 4°C temperature difference between your bed and ceiling return air. The AC compressor keeps running aggressively until the return air hits 24°C—leaving your sleeping area at a freezing 20°C.

BUILT-IN AC SENSOR: 24°C BED TEMP: 20°C (FREEZING)
PROBLEM 2 // HARDWARE CONTROL BARRIER

Wired Thermostats Don't Work

Traditional smart thermostats like Nest or Ecobee are built for central HVAC systems requiring 24V wired relay cables. Split ACs have zero wired thermostat ports.

To control a Split AC automatically, you need an Infrared (IR) based controller—which in reality is just a remote control. Basic IR blasters and mobile apps lack true local room sensing and occupancy radar.

NEST / ECOBEE: WIRED ONLY SPLIT AC: INFRARED ONLY
THE ACBOSS SOLUTION

Best of Both Worlds

We built ACBOSS to bridge this exact gap. It places a high-precision climate sensor right at bed/couch height—measuring true occupant thermal comfort—while using an onboard 360° IR array to send instant micro-adjustments to your Split AC. No wiring, no overcooling, no cloud latency.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about compatibility, local logic, and hardware setup.

Will ACBOSS work with my existing Split AC?

Yes. ACBOSS features a 38kHz IR receiver that learns your existing Split AC remote control signals during a 2-minute setup process.

What are the mounting options for ACBOSS?

ACBOSS offers flexible dual-mounting: place it flat on your bedside table or couch stand for bed-level sensing, or mount it cleanly on any wall using the included magnetic wall bracket or residue-free 3M adhesive strip.

Can ACBOSS run on a power bank during power outages?

Yes. ACBOSS is powered via a standard 5V DC USB-C port. You can plug it into any standard mobile power bank to maintain uninterrupted AC cooling autopilot during summer power cuts.

Does ACBOSS require active internet or Wi-Fi to run?

No. ACBOSS runs 100% of its comfort decision loops locally on the onboard MCU. Internet access is not required for continuous cooling autopilot.

What do early units look like?

Early units are custom 3D-printed hardware enclosures containing our fully-tested PCB prototype platform.
