Privacy policy.

A plain-language overview of how this website handles information.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

01 / SCOPE

About this policy.

This policy applies to the ROOT website operated by Haptique Electronics Pvt. Ltd. It describes the information we may receive when you browse the site, contact us, or join a linked community service such as Discord.

02 / INFORMATION

What we may receive.

When you use the contact form, you may choose to provide your name, email address, and message. The site may also receive basic technical information needed to deliver pages, such as browser type, device information, and server request data.

03 / USE

How we use information.

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, understand product feedback, maintain the website, and protect its security. We do not use the contact form to create a marketing mailing list unless we clearly ask for that separately.

04 / LINKS

Third-party services.

Links to Discord and other services take you to platforms operated by third parties. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this website. We do not control how those services process information.

05 / CHOICES

Your choices.

You can choose not to submit the contact form. You can also contact us to ask what information we hold about an enquiry or to request a correction, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification.

06 / CONTACT

Questions about privacy?

Email info@get-root.in with the subject “Privacy enquiry”. We will use the details you provide to respond to that request.

This page is written for transparency and general information. It is not legal advice. If the way we collect or use information changes, we will update this page.
