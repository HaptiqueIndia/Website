ABOUT ROOT

Comfort starts with the people behind it.

ROOT is built by founders who believe technology should make the room feel easier, not make life feel busier.

OUR STORY

From building products in France to rethinking comfort at home.

Willis Desai and Pradip Makwana founded ROOT after building a successful startup in France. Their work continued with the launch of Haptique in France through Kickstarter, bringing thoughtful hardware into people’s everyday spaces.

ROOT grows from that same belief: the best technology should understand the context around you, then quietly do its job.
